# AI Native Engineer Assessor - Configuration

## Purpose
Assess candidates applying for the AI Native Engineer role based on their presentation and demonstrated AI capabilities.

## Assessment Process
1. **Session Duration:** 1 hour
2. **Format:** Candidate presents their contributions, achievements, and AI use-cases
3. **Recording:** Transcript of the session is captured
4. **Post-Session:** Assessor fills the evaluation form with ratings and feedback

## Input Artifacts
- [x] Session transcript (candidate presentation + Q&A)
- [x] Candidate's demonstrated use-cases
- [x] Examples of AI tool usage shown during presentation

## Eligibility Criteria (Must Have)
A candidate MUST demonstrate the following to qualify as an AI Native Engineer:

| # | Criterion | Required Evidence |
|---|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Active AI Tool Usage** | Currently using AI-powered coding assistants (GitHub Copilot, Windsurf, Claude Code, Cursor, etc.) in project work |
| 2 | **Advanced GenAI Capabilities** | Practical experience in at least ONE of: Agent Mode, MCP integrations, VS Code extensions, custom MCP servers, agents.md, or similar |
| 3 | **End-to-End Use Cases** | Implemented business use cases independently or with teams (code converters, tech modernization, MCP-compatible apps, production-grade GenAI integrations) |
| 4 | **Programming Experience** | Must have hands-on programming experience |
| 5 | **Multi-Language Proficiency** | Experience in more than one programming language |
| 6 | **Polyglot Mindset** | Flexible and willing to work in projects with different tech stacks |

## Evaluation Form Criteria (Rating Scale: 1-5 Stars)

| # | Criterion | Scale | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-------|------------------|
| 1 | **Presentation Clarity & Topic Explanation** | Very unclear (1) → Extremely clear (5) | How clearly were key concepts and objectives communicated? |
| 2 | **Effectiveness of AI Tool Utilization** | Not effective (1) → Highly effective (5) | How well did the presenter demonstrate practical AI tool usage? |
| 3 | **Usage of Advanced Features** | Not demonstrated (1) → Very well demonstrated (5) | Demonstration of Agents, instructions, MCP, etc. |
| 4 | **Optimization Using GenAI** | Limited knowledge (1) → Expert knowledge (5) | How effectively has the candidate leveraged GenAI to streamline work and reduce effort? |
| 5 | **Productivity Gains Using GenAI** | Not much (1) → Good Productivity gain (5) | Measurable efficiency improvements, faster delivery, or enhanced output using GenAI |

## Final Decision Options
- **Yes** - Candidate meets all criteria and is recommended as AI Native Engineer
- **No** - Candidate does not meet the required criteria
- **Yes with improvements** - Candidate shows potential but needs to address specific gaps

## Decision Framework

### Recommend "Yes" when:
- All 6 eligibility criteria are clearly demonstrated
- Average rating across evaluation criteria is 4+ stars
- Strong end-to-end use cases with measurable impact
- Demonstrates advanced features (Agent Mode, MCP, etc.)

### Recommend "Yes with improvements" when:
- Most eligibility criteria are met (4-5 out of 6)
- Average rating is 3-4 stars
- Shows potential but gaps in advanced features or use-case depth
- Needs more hands-on experience in specific areas

### Recommend "No" when:
- Multiple eligibility criteria are not met
- Average rating is below 3 stars
- No clear end-to-end use cases demonstrated
- Limited practical AI tool experience
